# Courtyard frontage proportions (4 October 2026)

The owner's `proportions-reference.png` and `proportions-previous-model.png`
compare this garden frontage. The request is to refine its proportions
while retaining the established shapes. The images are architectural
references, not additional instructions. These dimensions supersede the
earlier proportions in README.md; they are visual estimates from an
oblique photograph rather than surveyed measurements.

The canted bay is now 4.8 units wide and 1.55 deep, with a 2.25-wide flat
front and 0.275-deep perpendicular returns. Its cheeks remain at 45 degrees.
The square pavilion narrows from 8.5 to 7.5, with its east wall and south
front retained. The adjoining blank projection widens from 2.05 to 3.05
so the two walls meet. The flat roof, coping and collision follow that width;
the roof-access door retains its threshold and remains exposed.

One close pair of sashes replaces the separated single openings on each
left-hand brick storey; a three-sash bank occupies the ground floor. The
upper bank beside the bay also has two distinct sashes. Bay cheek sashes
are narrower than the front sash, and the square pavilion's windows are
slightly shorter. The shared upper row moves from y=11 to 11.6, bringing
the openings closer to the photographed cornice. Main-range roof rise
falls from 3.78 to 1.65, square pavilion rise from 2.55 to 1.15, and the
bay roof rise from 0.98 to 0.5. Other bays retain their default roof rise.

Sources are `Browser/dist/east-photo-detail.mjs`, `escape-exterior.mjs`,
`redesmere-garden-photo-detail.mjs` and `west-refinement.mjs`, shared by
the browser game, Explore and aerial construction. Existing adjoining
garden-side openings, the courtyard fire escape, entrance and passage
remain attached. Saved source/compiled/Explore review views and actual
walking measurements use `Browser/artifacts/redesmere-proportions/`.
The exact spatial comparison verifies 1,444,515 primitives outside the
facade correction area remain unchanged, with 30 net local primitives.
Unity, Blender and packaged desktop/Android exports are not regenerated.
