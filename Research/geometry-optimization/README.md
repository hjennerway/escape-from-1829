# Geometry and interior loading — 6 October 2026

The owner selected geometry-audit proposals 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8 and 9, then confirmed that the finished interior could proceed with gameplay TODO 7. These changes concern browser rendering and loading; they do not alter the historical plan or regenerate Unity/Blender exports.

1. Compiled scene geometry shares byte-identical typed buffers, including exact normals/UVs and signed zero. Hash collisions are checked byte for byte. Mutable instance matrices and colours remain independent. The final aerial build avoids 41,345,678 bytes of duplicate geometry buffers.
2. Round-headed corridor and Oakmere court window assemblies batch within their existing building/period boundaries. Distant window atlases rasterize their actual projected triangles and glass mask. Nearby windows retain their original geometry and shadow policy.
3. Windsor chairs and Panca benches retain their complete source geometry nearby. The offline distant versions contain 1,710 versus 4,888 chair triangles and 1,300 versus 3,720 bench triangles. Simplifier relative errors are 0.001423 and 0.000182, below the 0.004 limit. Original normalization bounds, placements and collision dimensions stay fixed. Room/spatial batches permit independent culling. Source/output hashes and the existing MIT/GPL-3.0 licences are retained in the furniture LOD manifest.
5. Roof support surveys cache transformed geometry and boundary records between finishing passes. Direct position/index/instance edits and world-transform changes invalidate their records. The cache is released after final finishing. This reuses the current roof-to-render repair; original slate/support geometry is retained.
6. Cell padding indexes only exactly equal complete vertices. It preserves all 65,722 source triangles, relief, normals and UV seams. Room meshes have independent bounds; section compilation can divide a room mesh further at its doorway plane without changing its surface.
8. The pharmacy gas holder retains its original nearby shell, ribs and bands. Beyond 95 metres it uses the same shell silhouette, a corrugation normal texture and smaller torus bands, with hysteresis. Cap, posts, base and collision footprint remain outside the switch.
9. Kerb centreline rendering uses a 5 mm maximum deviation with exact endpoints. Recorded centreline/navigation data remains unchanged: 244 samples become 158, and the triangular kerb's 137 samples become 55. Oakmere court windows use the shared assembly batching and atlas controller.

Close-up padding, furniture, glass/roof and gas-holder checks retain the full nearby detail.

The ten interior render sections use existing transverse doorway planes: ground/first-floor wings and centre, basement west/east, and upper-floor west/east. Basement and upper-floor cuts follow their actual occupied footprint rather than estate symmetry. Clipping interpolates every vertex attribute and assigns coplanar cut faces to one section. An independent complete-surface-area comparison protects against missing or duplicate geometry. Navigation, collisions, door/stair records, NPC routes and interaction state continue to use the complete plan while rendering loads. Shared live finishes retain the basement mural shader; its supplied image is unchanged.

The prebuilt sections and four floor label resources total about 2.68 MB compressed. The same compiler runs in a worker as fallback. Entry requires its section and nearby corridor/stair connections; remaining sections prepare in distance order and stay cached. Main-thread restoration, furniture, shader preparation and GPU uploads yield between stages. A failed preparation remains gated and retryable, including worker-launch failure. Replay updates loaded furniture and lets future sections use the latest placement seed. Receipts include desktop/mobile frame pacing and memory; timings are local diagnostic measurements on an RTX 3090 Ti, not mobile-device benchmarks or a guarantee against driver/shader stalls.
