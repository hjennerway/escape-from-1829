# Estates department

The user-supplied `img1.jpg` shows the courtyard entrance. The red dot and
arrow in `img1-loc.png` identify its position and viewing direction only.
They are not physical features.

The building shape follows the existing brown OS outline: building 2,
contour 1 in `Browser/dist/historic-footprint-data.mjs`, transformed by the
existing `historicOSPoint` registration. Its stepped U occupies approximately
x=233.79..253.94, z=-62.76..-36.32. The opening faces the service road towards
negative X, near Irby/Ashley. The later clockwise alignment described below supersedes this original placement.

The photo guides the two-storey rear offices, tall right-hand gabled return,
lower hipped front room, left workshop and entrance wall, red brick bands,
slate roofs with terracotta ridges, white glazing, turquoise doors and
rainwater goods, blue department plaques and cobbled courtyard. Heights,
internal divisions and concealed elevations are estimates from one photograph.
The OS outline determines the footprint where the photograph is obscured.

The Estates department belongs to Historic and the browser walking/gameplay
estate. Its courtyard and entrance remain accessible, with collisions on the
building masses. Only this selected brown outline is retired. The small
detached outline beside it remains unmodelled.

Choose **Estates department** in Locations, or open `aerial.html?view=estates`,
`?view=estates-plan`, `?view=estates-photo`, `?view=estates-site`, or
`explore.html?view=estates`. Unity and Blender exports are unchanged.

The existing Main/admin rear-court viewpoint moves slightly into the Estates
gateway, keeping both its walking start and sightline clear of the new walls.

## Clockwise alignment correction

The red-circled building in `clockwise-alignment.png` is rotated 19 degrees
clockwise about the vertical axis. Its centre is now x=252.8, z=-49.5, placing
the tower-facing edge along the yellow guide while keeping its corners clear
of the surrounding service roads. The angle and alignment are estimates from
the marked perspective view. This supersedes the original OS orientation; all
wall lengths, heights, roofs and details retain their dimensions. The courtyard,
walking collisions, footprint metadata and photo/walking viewpoints follow the
same rigid transform. The north-up plan and site view retain their map axes.

## Service court and sideways alignment

building 6.5 units towards the tower, to x=246.3, z=-49.5. The 19-degree
