# West render bands and cornice corners

The owner's [marked game view](../../../Browser/artifacts/west-render-corners/marked-reference.png),
supplied on 4 October 2026, identifies a doubled horizontal strip on the
recessed garden pavilion in yellow, and untidy outer-end joins in blue.
The written request asks for an even strip and clean corners, with a check
for the same problem elsewhere.

The low forward root's cornice and roof support projected through the taller
pavilion's west face. Their trim footprint now excludes that pavilion and
the existing band's projection, leaving one white strip across the marked
face. Masonry, glazing, collision outlines and slate pitches retain their
definitions.

The outer-end bands previously used y=4.08 / 8.8 while the adjoining front
and court used y=4.05 / 8.6. The replacement follows the court steps, both
returns of the shallow entrance pier, the outer front and the garden bays
as one continuous course at each of the latter levels. Its height is 0.18,
matching the neighbouring straight bands. The outer cornice's three profiles
also follow both pier returns and the front corner at a common level; the
central gutter follows that level. The middle garden bay's two upper cornices
continue through its straight stem and canted corners as joined strips.
These are corrections to the current model rather than survey measurements.

The complete facade audit covers 22 swept courses, 117 explicit corners and
282 shared ward/courtyard/shop corners. It also probes the full scene at the
marked end and garden faces, detecting separate competing strips that an
individual-mesh audit would miss. Restoring the original trim builders fails
the new end-corner regression. Matching before/after and actual browser views
are in [Browser/artifacts/west-render-corners](../../../Browser/artifacts/west-render-corners/).

The shared browser exterior supplies aerial, Explore and game geometry.
Local compiled aerial assets are regenerated. Unity, Blender and packaged
desktop/Android exports are not regenerated. See DEVELOPMENT.md for final
validation results and limitations.
