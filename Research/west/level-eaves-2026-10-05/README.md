# Level west-wing roof-to-wall connections

The owner's [blue-circled/red-wall reference](../../../Browser/artifacts/west-level-eaves/level-eaves-reference.png)
on 5 October 2026 identifies the height mismatch as the cause of the broken
roof joins. All roof-to-wall edges inside the circle must match the marked
main wall. This supersedes the raised eaves and descending rendered returns
in the earlier roof-eaves and roof-render-joins revisions, and the unfinished
four-bay connection repair. The annotations are modelling reference evidence.

The shared west-range plan sets the supporting walls to y=14.3 and slate
eaves to y=14.53. The outer pavilion/end and both canted bays now use that
same level as the main range and inner pavilion. Upper cornice caps and
gutters follow it. The outer eaves lower from 15.47, court-bay eaves from
15.5, and garden-bay eaves from 15.1. These are fitted model coordinates,
rather than surveyed dimensions.

The main ridge and four branches retain their positions and y=17.08 crowns.
The roof pitches descend directly to a level perimeter. Raised shoulders,
short slate wedges, white risers/returns and their extra volume clipping are
removed. The entrance-side ridge and its render-edge correction are retained.
Wall footprints, windows, doors, lower floor bands, stairs and low flat decks
retain their definitions; only upper wall/cornice heights change.

Sources are shared by browser aerial, Explore and gameplay. The local compiled
aerial model is regenerated. Unity, Blender and packaged exports are not
regenerated. Validation is limited to 1829 and its immediate surroundings,
as requested. Focused regressions, saved inputs, source/compiled close and
overhead views, GPU/build logs and model metadata are kept in
`Browser/artifacts/west-level-eaves/`. Final results are in `DEVELOPMENT.md`.
