# Courtyard bay protrusion: clarified location (5 October 2026)

The owner's [wider locator](../../../Browser/artifacts/west-courtyard-roof-tip/wider-reference.png)
clarifies that the original [close screenshot](../../../Browser/artifacts/west-courtyard-roof-tip/close-reference.png)
marks the west shoulder of the **courtyard polygonal bay**, beside the low
flat-roof projection. The earlier pavilion-roof-tip work targeted the garden
side and did not resolve this reported defect. The images locate the defect;
the written request asks for its removal.

The unified bay roof had replaced nearly all of the old return hip, leaving
a 0.1725-unit-wide slate sliver at x=-62.05..-61.8775. That sliver projected
beyond the joined roof shoulder. The separate white return strip at x=-61.72
also extended past the bay's cornice. The old hip is now removed after its
original surfaces have been used for roof-join sampling, and the obsolete
white strip is omitted. The joined roof, masonry and bay cornice retain
their established profiles.

The shared browser changes are in `west-cross-range-roof.mjs` and
`west-court-photo-detail.mjs`. `test-west-roof-join.mjs` adds nine physical
roof rays and four trim-clearance rays at the clarified location; the saved
former geometry fails the new regression. Hardware source/compiled captures
include an overview, the complete bay, close/opposite angles and phone.
Unity, Blender and packaged application exports are not regenerated.
