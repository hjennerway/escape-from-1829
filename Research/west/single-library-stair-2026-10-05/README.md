# Single Library access staircase — 5 October 2026

The owner's [green-circled stair screenshot](reference.png) requests removal
of the whole upper flight/landing assembly and one staircase in the blue-arrow
direction. This supersedes the earlier diagonal return documented in
[the upper-access notes](../upper-access-stair-2026-10-05/README.md).

S5's first-to-Library connection (plan IDs 1 → 3, Y=4.2 → 8.4) is now a single
straight flight beside the west edge of the retained lower well. It rises north,
along the corridor direction shown by the arrow, from (-34.85,4.2,13.5) to
(-34.85,8.4,8.35). The landing portals are at Z=14.15 and Z=7.7. Its 1.3-unit
width, 5.15-unit run and 24 risers are modelling estimates from the annotation,
not surveyed architectural measurements.

Both former upper flights, the diagonal return, broad middle landing and their
rails are removed. The old square opening is closed at the Library storey;
the new narrow opening, concrete flight, treads, rails, walking and navigation
share the same connection geometry. R50's east partition has a local 0.8-unit
setback beside the new flight, returning to its prior line at Z=14.2. C26 meets
the new top landing. The wider Library passage and exterior outline remain.

Basement-to-ground and ground-to-first connections retain their routes and
flight geometry. The old well's first-floor guard closes its unused ascent
mouth. Library stair markers and the developer overlay follow the new flight.
Both shared JSON plans and the first-floor/Library review drawings are updated.
