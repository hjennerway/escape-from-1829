# Hampton / Ince courtyard windows and low roof

The owner's [green/red/purple/yellow annotated game view](../../../Browser/artifacts/west-court-window-roof/marked-reference.png),
supplied on 4 October 2026, identifies the recessed lower window pairs beside
the courtyard polygonal bay, its six narrow canted sashes, and the small roof
over the adjoining stair projection. The written request defines the changes;
the screenshot locates them.

Each green lower pair becomes one 0.78 x 2.25 sash. Both share x=-62.65,
halfway across the exposed recessed wall between the polygonal bay's edge
x=-61.65 and the stair projection's edge x=-63.65. Their existing y=2 and
6.4 floor positions remain. The unmarked upper recessed pair and the four
broader stair-projection windows retain their dimensions and positions.

All six red canted bay sashes now have 1.30-unit glazing width, matching the
purple central sash on each of the three floors. They retain their wall
orientations, centres, 2.50-unit height and pane divisions. This supersedes
the court bay's earlier 0.72-unit side widths; the separate garden-side
window-alignment correction remains in place.

The yellow roof becomes a flat slate slab, seated directly on the existing
8.65-high masonry. Its deck is at y=8.81 and the pale rim rises only 0.20
above it. A small 0.10 overhang covers each side. The short roof downpipe at
x=-66.55 and the adjacent overlapping run at x=-65.90 are removed: keeping
the latter would leave the projection's sash bank obstructed. The far end
pipe at x=-71.70 remains.
