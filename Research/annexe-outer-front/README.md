# Outer annexe front elevations

The yellow circles in `img1-loc.png` identify the outer portions of the front connecting ranges and their end wards, beyond the courtyard wings. `img1.jpg` supplies the elevation. Both receive a reflected version of the same design, as requested for the two red approach arrows.

The browser model adds projecting three-window gables with circular vents, terracotta verges and bands, tall divided sashes, a recessed arched entrance, a low roofed entrance room, downpipes and a taller chimney. Existing front windows behind the replacement are suppressed. Dimensions and unseen details are approximate; glazing represents intact windows rather than the photograph's boarded condition. The registered range bodies and site placement are retained. Blender and Unity assets are unchanged.

Choose **Annexe outer front · West/East** under Locations, or use `aerial.html?view=annexe-outer-west` and `aerial.html?view=annexe-outer-east`. The same keys work in `explore.html`.

The September 17 red/blue annotation copies this paired apex design onto the
forward faces of Tarvin/Jarman and Picton/Carden. Each receives two projecting
three-window bays, brick gables, slate cross roofs, circular vents and the same
terracotta detailing. The elevation fits each existing courtyard range's width
and belongs to its ward; the original outer fronts are retained. The separate
outer entrance rooms and arches are not part of the copied apex sections.
Generic front windows on both the main range and its corner returns are replaced
to prevent overlapping glazing. The new projections have walking collisions.

Use the **Tarvin/Jarman** and **Picton/Carden** Locations entries for inspection.
`Browser/test-annexe-outer-front.mjs` checks the copied geometry, exposed windows,
clear approaches and collisions. The approved annexe geometry snapshot now
includes these requested fronts; central entrance geometry and site placement
are unchanged. Before/after views are in `Browser/artifacts/annexe-court-fronts-*`.

The later September 24 Jarman photographs supersede the copied west court
frontage only; see [Jarman lawn frontage](../jarman/README.md). The east court
and both outer elevations retain the design described above.
